or trade business. The client corrected this: **BasicBlend works with everyone** — businesses just
starting out, growing companies, established brands, product businesses and software/service
businesses including SaaS. The catalogue's own copy agrees ("whether you're a startup or an
established brand"; "businesses of all sizes"). The portfolio is a sample, not the audience.

## 3. Ideal customers

## 4. Value proposition

- **Functional** — one team covering shoot, creative, copy, paid media, web and automation, so the
  chain doesn't break between vendors.
- **Business** — work built around the client's stage and market, not a packaged playbook.
- **Emotional** — being understood: a team that learns the business and writes in the language its
  customers speak.
- **Proof / credibility** — named clients with visible deliverables, campaign creative, production
  films, and a studio you can walk into.

## 11. Brand personality & tone of voice

**Derived, not chosen.** The wordmark is heavy, wide, cream-on-black poster type inside a stamped
rectangular frame, with a lightning bolt struck through the *I*. The catalogue is louder still —
acid green, graffiti script, a classical statue at a laptop, a Drake meme inside real client work.
The delivered work is practical, product-first and unpretentious.

**Personality:** bold · practical · clear · a bit street · quick · unpretentious.
**Not:** corporate, delicate, luxury-minimal, or "cutting-edge innovator".

**Tone of voice**

- Short, declarative sentences. Concrete nouns over category words.
- Name the real thing rather than the category: "we shot this in a working kitchen", not "immersive
  brand experiences".
- Hinglish where it's genuinely theirs, never as decoration.
- Banned from copy: *synergy, leverage, holistic, cutting-edge, solutions, transform your business,
  take it to the next level, we are passionate about*.
- Confident, never boastful — we have no numbers, so specificity does the persuading.

## 12. Conflicts found & how each was resolved

1. **Identity conflict.** The catalogue cover uses a gradient "B" monogram on plum with acid-lime
   and lilac; `logo.PNG` is a black/cream wordmark with a lilac bolt. → **`logo.PNG` wins** as
   primary: it is the standalone, purpose-supplied logo asset and the most recently modified file.
   Its palette drives the site. The gradient monogram is retired. The catalogue's lilac survives as
   the accent (both sources agree on lilac) and its plum survives as a third ground.
2. **Social handle.** Catalogue says `Basicblend07`; the contact sheet says `basicblend007` and the
   email is `basicblend007@gmail.com`. → **`@basicblend007`** — two sources plus the live URL agree.
3. **Legal placeholders.** The T&C/Privacy contain `[Insert Website URL]`, `[Insert
   Jurisdiction/State/Country]`, and a late-payment clause reading *"a late fee of per month"* with
   the figure missing. → URL filled with the deployed domain (single config value); jurisdiction set
   to **Chandigarh, India**, defensible from the registered address; **the missing percentage is NOT
   invented** — the clause reads "a late fee as set out in your service agreement". Flagged in §14.
4. **Service list mismatch.** Catalogue omits GMB, hoardings, lead generation and product shoots
   that the services sheet lists. → Union taken; catalogue's grouping kept (§5).
5. **Typos in source.** "OUR VISSION", "Al & Agents" (OCR of AI), "Catelog". → Corrected silently.
6. **Google Business Profile.** The contact sheet has the label but no URL. → Only the Google Maps
   link is used; no GBP link is fabricated.

## 13. Website objectives

1. Say what BasicBlend does, and that it works with businesses of every kind, within seconds.
2. Give each service its own page that can rank and receive ad traffic.
3. Make the work tangible at a useful size, on dedicated case pages.

## 14. Assumptions & unresolved questions

**Assumptions made (documented so they can be corrected):**

1. `logo.PNG` is the current identity. The client's stated brand colours (black, purple, orange)
   are used around it; the logo itself is unmodified.
2. The six clients may be named publicly. Their work was supplied inside a sales catalogue clearly
   intended to be shown to prospects, which implies consent to display.
3. Jurisdiction for the legal pages is Chandigarh, India, inferred from the registered address.
4. `SITE_URL` in `src/data/site.ts` is a placeholder (`https://basicblend.netlify.app`) until the
   real domain is known. It feeds canonical URLs, OG tags, sitemap and the legal pages — **one value
   to change.**
5. The contact form uses Netlify Forms because the deploy target is Netlify and no backend was
   specified. WhatsApp, phone and email are offered alongside, so the page converts even if forms
   are not enabled.
6. Business hours are not stated anywhere, so none are published.

**Resolved during the build review** (recorded so the reasoning is not lost):

- The hero originally carried a `Chandigarh · Sector 22-D` eyebrow above the headline. Removed —
  that shape is the default AI hero. The city now leads the hero's opening sentence.
- Capability and sector rows originally carried `01`–`07` indices. Removed; numbering is kept only
  where it is genuinely ordinal (the process steps, the case plates).
- Ten pieces of metadata had grown into sentences set in letterspaced mono caps. They now use a
  readable `.meta` style; `.spec` is reserved for true labels.

**Open questions for the client:**

1. What is the real domain?
2. The late-payment percentage in the T&C, and confirmation of governing jurisdiction.
3. May we name all six clients publicly? Do any have verified results we could publish? Are there
   more projects (especially websites, platforms or SaaS work) we could add as case studies?
4. Any testimonial, even a WhatsApp message we could quote with permission?
5. Founding year and team size — both would add credibility and are currently absent.
6. Is there a Google Business Profile URL, and higher-resolution masters for the videos?
7. Is `Orwo Flame` a Maa Sharda sub-brand or a separate client? Both logos appear on the same
   creatives; the site currently presents it as a Maa Sharda product line.
